Incident Response Runbook

Document ID: OPS-RUN-002 · Owner: Sarah Iyer · Version 3.1.

Severity levels

  • Sev 1: Customer-facing outage or confirmed data breach. Page on-call within 5 minutes; incident commander assigned; status page updated every 30 minutes.
  • Sev 2: Degraded service for some customers. Acknowledge within 15 minutes.
  • Sev 3: Internal impact only. Next business day.

Breach handling

Suspected data breaches go to the Security Lead immediately. CERT-In must be notified within 6 hours of detection. Preserve logs; do not rotate affected keys until forensics has a snapshot, unless the key is actively being abused.

Post-incident

A blameless postmortem is due within 5 business days, with action items tracked as Jira tickets in the owning team's project.
