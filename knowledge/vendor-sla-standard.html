Vendor SLA Standard

Document ID: VEN-POL-003 · Version 2.0 · Owner: Sarah Iyer (Operations Lead) · Effective: 1 April 2026.

Minimum service levels for any vendor that hosts, processes or transmits Acme customer data or sits on a customer-facing request path.

RequirementMinimum standard
Monthly availability (uptime)99.95% or higher
Severity 1 incident responseWithin 30 minutes, 24x7
Severity 1 resolution or workaroundWithin 4 hours
Breach notification to AcmeWithin 6 hours of discovery (aligned with CERT-In)
Service creditsAt least 10% of the monthly fee below 99.95%, 25% below 99.0%
Recovery point objective (RPO)15 minutes or less
Recovery time objective (RTO)2 hours or less
Data residencyIndia regions only for customer personal data (see SEC-POL-001 section 5)

Contracts that do not meet these minimums require sign-off from the Operations Lead and the Security Lead before signature.
